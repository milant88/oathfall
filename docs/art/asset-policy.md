# Asset and art policy

Prototype assets may come from approved free sources. Record every imported asset in an asset manifest with source URL, license, download date, hash or version, modifications, intended use, and reviewer.

Keep third-party originals separate from Oathfall-owned prefabs, materials, and variants. Validate humanoid rigs, avatar assignment, scale, weapon sockets, animation transitions, texture compression, URP materials, LODs, and collision before content use. Default textures to 1K–2K unless visible benefit justifies more.
