# Hollowwood expedition — implemented design, revision 4

Twelve authored stops form a continuous route of roughly 600 m including lateral movement. Movement, positioning, looting and equipment choices remain player-controlled. Windows offline, Dawnwarden only.

| Stop | Objective | Authored groups / time |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Approach | Teach blade range and dodge | 2 groups of 4 Raiders |
| Abandoned Camp | Mixed enemy roles | 3 groups of 5 |
| Fallen Oath Shrine | Hold ward and clear remaining threats | 75 active seconds |
| Mossfall Quarry | Fight among ruined masonry | 3 groups of 5 |
| Broken Watch | Prioritize spellcasters and heavy attacks | 3 groups of 5 |
| Whispering Grove | Hold ward and clear remaining threats | 90 active seconds |
| Pilgrim Crossing | Recovery and equipment decision | Health/draught refill, equipment |
| Lost Caravan | Sustained mixed engagement | 4 groups of 5 |
| Silent Bell Court | Hold ward and clear remaining threats | 105 active seconds |
| Oathkeeper Gate | Final mixed gauntlet | 4 groups of 6 |
| Last Vigil | Prepare for guardian | Health/draught refill, equipment |
| Crown of Roots | Defeat Fallen Oathkeeper | Additional rune pattern below 55% health |

Ward progress requires the living player within 9 m; threats continue to spawn while incomplete, capped at six active enemies. Leaving the circle stops progress. Completion also requires remaining enemies defeated. There is no passive wait-to-win path. Skirmish groups have a 1.25-second breather after clearance. Sanctuaries complete on arrival and do not impose a waiting timer.

Raider uses sampled sword contact; Hexblade marks a delayed targeted circle; Breaker marks a larger nearby strike; Guardian changes its pattern below 55% health. Rune strikes respect world obstruction and cancel on caster death or stagger. Role visuals are derivatives of one approved humanoid foundation, not bespoke finished creatures.

Three restorative draughts heal 65 with an 8-second reuse interval. Securing a stop heals 45 and refills one draught; sanctuaries restore health and all three draughts. Regular drops occur every fourth defeated enemy, with checkpoint/guardian rewards. Inventory holds 24 items, manual equipment and explicit disposal; discarded IDs stay claimed to prevent duplicate collection.

Pacing target: 600–900 seconds of active play. Completed player/audit timing belongs in the quality report, not this target table. Repetition, enemy variety and human first-play time need external playtesting even when an automated run lands in this interval.
