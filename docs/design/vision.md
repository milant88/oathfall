# Product vision

Oathfall is an accessible, tactical isometric action roguelite. A 30–45 minute expedition connects handcrafted rooms through visible reward branches. Combat rewards positioning, readable telegraphs, dodging, blocking, and build-changing equipment.

The launch target is three heroes, three acts, three bosses, twelve enemy archetypes, 48 equipment bases, 90 affixes, and 24 relics. The first playable prototype deliberately proves the Dawnwarden, combat clarity, and deterministic loot before broad content work.

## Pillars

1. Responsive combat before content volume.
2. Items alter behavior as well as values.
3. Enemy combinations create legible tactical problems.
4. Route choices expose risk and reward.
5. Data-driven content is reproducible and safe to extend.

## Hero contract

Every hero has a basic attack, defensive action, movement skill, two active abilities, ultimate, resource, and upgrade paths. Dawnwarden uses Resolve and sword-and-shield play; Thornstrider uses Focus and marked targets; Runebinder uses mana and elemental marks.

## Run contract

Run equipment, temporary powers, gold, and corruption reset after a run. Profile discoveries, recipes, and capped permanent progression persist. Oaths grant a strong reward and a visible drawback; corruption improves rewards while strengthening later encounters.
